# Seedance Camera — the 50-shot treatment library

Use this skill whenever a shot needs its camera decided or phrased — for a Seedance
2.0/2.5 prompt, a storyboard panel, or a shot list. The library below is the
vocabulary: pick ONE treatment per shot, write it with these exact terms, and drop
the `cinematography` line into the prompt's look/style block (never re-stated per
segment).

## How to choose

- **Scale** answers "how much of the world is in frame" — establishers wide, emotion
  close; sizes progress with intensity across a scene.
- **Angle** answers power and point of view — low = imposing, high = vulnerable,
  POV/OTS = alignment with a character.
- **Movement** answers energy — static lock-offs hold and observe; pushes commit;
  follows and orbits give momentum. A shot's END composition is where its move
  lands: static shots cannot end reframed, moving shots must.
- **Composition** arranges subjects in the frame; **Specialty** covers signature
  techniques.
- ONE camera treatment per shot (or per timestamped segment on Seedance 2.5). Stacked
  or contradictory camera notes cause drifting framing.
- Basic terms are written raw; techniques (long take, dolly zoom, FPV, bullet time,
  speed ramp) are written by name; anything niche becomes `[term + plain
  description]` — e.g. "Rack focus: the focus shifts smoothly — the foreground blurs
  as the character sharpens."
- A transition names its trigger AND method ("at 5s, a quick left wipe with a natural
  dissolve") and never dangles.

## Output

When asked to pick, answer with the treatment's **name**, its `framing · angle · move`
line for the shot header, and the **cinematography line** for the style block. When
asked to phrase camera inside action text, use the framing/angle/move terms verbatim
and keep lens/light language out of the action — it lives once, in the style block.
